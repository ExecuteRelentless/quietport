Private, invite only

A shared folder no one else can read.

Quietport puts a folder on the computers of the people you invite. Everything in it is locked before it leaves your Mac or PC. The server in the middle never sees a thing.

See how it works

Drop in a file. It is locked before it leaves your computer and unlocks only for the people in that folder.

How it works

3 steps. No account.

Step 1

Get a link.

Someone invites you. The link is made just for you, works once, and has 1 button.

https://quietport.app/j/fbco4tqe5qh5wwfibe2oiuypfu
Step 2

Open. Click Continue.

No sign-up. No password. Nothing to type. About a minute.

Quietport will set up your shared folder now.

It takes about a minute and needs no password.

CancelContinue
Quietport is connected.

Your shared folder is called QPSync and is pinned in the Finder sidebar.

OKOpen Folder
Step 3

Your folder is ready.

It shows up in Finder or File Explorer, 1 folder for each circle you are in. It works like any other folder. There is no app to learn.

Circles

1 circle. 1 folder. 1 key.

A circle is a folder shared with the people you choose. Make as many as you like. Be in as many as you like. Each one is its own folder on your computer. A circle you are not in does not exist for you.
