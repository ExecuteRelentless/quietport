Private, invite only

A shared folder no one else can read.

Quietport creates a shared folder on the computers of the people you have invited. What goes in it is encrypted before it leaves your machine, so the server in the middle cannot decrypt anything it stores.

See how it works

Drop a file in. It is locked on your computer, passes through the hub as gibberish, and unlocks only inside the same folder on the other side.

How it works

3 steps, no account.

Step 1

Someone sends you a link.

Every invite is made for 1 person and carries only the folders meant for them. It works once and expires after 24 hours.

https://quietport.app/j/fbco4tqe5qh5wwfibe2oiuypfu
Step 2

Paste 1 line.

There is nothing to sign up for. You paste 1 line from your invite link and Quietport installs itself in less than 60 seconds. No administrator password, nothing outside your own user folder.

$ curl -fsSL https://quietport.app/j/fbco4tqe…/mac | sh
Quietport is connected.
$ _
Step 3

A folder appears.

You don't need to learn a special application, because there isn't one. QPSync shows up in Finder or File Explorer with 1 folder per circle you were invited to, and it works like any other folder on your computer.

Circles

1 circle, 1 folder, 1 key.

Sharing is organised in circles. A circle is 1 shared folder with 1 list of members and 1 key of its own. Run as many as you like. A person can belong to several, and each circle they are in shows up as its own folder. A circle they are not in does not show up at all, and nothing on their machine can tell it exists.
