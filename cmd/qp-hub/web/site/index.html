a shared folder no one else can read.

Quietport creates a shared folder on the computers of the people you have invited. What goes in it is encrypted before it leaves your machine, so the server in the middle cannot decrypt anything it stores.

Download
  • Nothing to sign up for

    You paste 1 line from your invite link and Quietport installs itself in less than 60 seconds as a new folder in Finder or File Explorer.

  • No app to learn

    You don't need to learn a special application, because there isn't one. It works like any other folder on your computer.

  • A server that reads nothing

    The only thing the server does is store ciphertext.

Sharing is organised in circles.

A circle is 1 shared folder with 1 list of members and 1 key of its own. Run as many as you like.

A person can belong to several, and each circle they are in shows up as its own folder on their computer. A circle they are not in does not show up at all, and nothing on their machine can tell it exists.
