A shared folder no one else can read.

Quietport puts a folder on the computers of the people you invite. Everything is encrypted before it goes anywhere, so the server in the middle never sees a thing.

DownloadHow it works

Drop in a file, or they do. It is locked before it leaves either computer and unlocks only for the people in that folder.

1 circle. 1 folder. 1 key.

A circle is a folder shared with the people you choose. Make as many as you want, be part of as many as you wish. Each one appears as its own folder on your computer. A circle you are not part of does not exist on your computer.
